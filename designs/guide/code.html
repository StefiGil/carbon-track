User Guide: How to prepare and upload your data

Follow these three simple steps to ensure your environmental reporting is accurate, compliant, and ready for institutional analysis.

1

Preparation of consumption data

Ensure your Excel file has these columns accurately formatted: year, month, electricity_kwh, gas_m3, fuel_l.

2

Emission Factors

Your Excel should include specific calculation constants: year, electricity_factor, gas_factor, fuel_factor.

info

Note: You can find these factors at the Secretaría de Energía.

3

Download your prepared data

Once you have accurately filled in all the required information in the templates, download your Excel files. These files are now ready for institutional reporting and analysis.
